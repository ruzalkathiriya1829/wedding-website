Your Journey to Our Celebration

Every beautiful journey deserves a wonderful destination. Here’s everything you need to know to reach us.

Mercure Goa Devaaya Resort

Divar Island, Goa

1. Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX) — ~40 km (approx. 50 mins drive). Best for most domestic flights.

2. Dabolim Airport, South Goa (GOI) — ~35 km (approx. 45 mins drive).

Prepaid airport taxis are readily available at both airports to the Ribandar ferry terminal.

1. Karmali Railway Station (KRMI) — ~10 km (Closest station, ~15 mins to the ferry).

2. Thivim Railway Station (THVM) — ~25 km (approx. 40 mins).

3. Madgaon Junction (MAO) — ~40 km (approx. 50 mins).

Station taxis are available directly to the Ribandar or Old Goa ferry jetty.

The Scenic Ferry Crossing: Divar Island is connected by round-the-clock roll-on/roll-off government car ferries across the Mandovi river (free for pedestrians and cars, every 15 mins).

Ferry Terminals:

  • Ribandar Ferry Wharf (from Panjim side) — 5 mins ferry ride to Divar Island.
  • Old Goa Ferry Wharf (from Old Goa churches) — 5 mins ferry ride.

You can drive your car straight onto the ferry and drive off directly onto the island!
